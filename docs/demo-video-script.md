# FarmTori: 3-minute pitch demo

Total length 3:00. Narration is about 390 words (130 words a minute). Record the screen first, then record the voice over it.

## Before you record (30 minutes)

1. Run `supabase/apply_all_pending.sql` in the Supabase SQL editor. Set `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` on the admin site.
2. Use a clean demo farmer. Register everything live or from real entries. Do not show any made-up data.
3. Prepare ONE farm with: a rice season, 4 to 5 activities with weather, one harvest with 3 photos, one expense in USD and one in LRD, and last year's records so "Farm record by year" has content.
4. Open these four things in advance, in this order:
   - The phone, screen recording on (iPhone: Control Centre. Android: Quick Settings). Turn on Do Not Disturb.
   - The USSD simulator in a browser tab (`server/` simulator).
   - The buyer page `/b/{code}` in a browser on a second phone, or a second browser tab.
   - The admin dashboard, logged in.
5. Put the phone in aeroplane mode for the offline shot (scene 3), then turn Wi-Fi back on for the sync shot.
6. Test the QR scan once before filming.

## Recording tips

- Record each scene separately, 2 to 3 takes. Cut between them. Short scenes hide small mistakes.
- Keep taps slow and deliberate. Do not scroll fast. Pause 1 second on each result so viewers can read it.
- Zoom in on the phone (crop to the phone in your editor) so text is readable on a projector.
- Record the voice last, in a quiet room, reading from the table above. Do not talk while tapping.
- Add large captions for each scene title (for example "Works offline", "Buyer scans the QR"). Many people watch with the sound off.
- Free editors: CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or Clipchamp. Export 1080p, MP4.

## If you run short of time, cut in this order

1. The profit details (keep only the profit card).
2. The plan-next-season shot.
3. The admin dashboard (keep one 5-second shot).

## What not to claim

- Do not say "verified", "certified" or "guaranteed". Say the data is "entered by the farmer". "Approved" appears only when an inspector has approved a batch.
- Do not claim it has been tested with farmers in the field unless it has.
- Do not mention the live app features I have not tested on a device. Test every scene on your phone before you film.
